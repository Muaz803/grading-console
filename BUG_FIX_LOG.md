# Grading Console — Bug Fix Log

Only bugs that were actually fixed are listed. Numbers (#) refer to findings in `BUG_AUDIT.md`.

**Test method.** The app's `<script>` was extracted verbatim from the edited HTML and run in Node.js v24 with a mocked DOM.
- **Round 1 (#1–#16):** 41 targeted checks, plus 9 end-to-end checks that parse genuine `.xls` files with the real SheetJS library. All passed.
- **Round 2 (remaining essential bugs):** 38 further checks, including real `.xlsx` and corrupt-file tests with SheetJS 0.20.3. That copy is byte-identical to the pinned CDN file (same SHA-384). All passed.
- After round 2, all 88 checks were re-run together and all passed.

Visual rendering in a real browser was not tested.

| **#9** Histogram bars were not scaled; bins with more than 17 students ran off the chart. | 200 students in the 60–70 bin: bar height 2400 px on a 240 px canvas. | Fixed `h = v*12` pixels per student. | Bar height is scaled to the largest bin: `h = v/maxBin*190`. | 200 in one bin: tallest bar 190 px, top inside canvas. | **#10** Missing or misnamed columns failed silently (stats `undefined`/`NaN`, empty CSV). | Header `Total marks` or `Marks`: upload "worked" but everything was blank or NaN, with no message. | Exact hard-coded keys and no header check. | `parseRows()` matches the required headers ignoring case and surrounding spaces. | **#11** Blank or non-numeric marks produced `undefined`/`NaN` stats and those students were dropped. | Marks `AB` and a blank cell: Min label `undefined`, Avg `NaN`, rows missing from CSV. | No type validation on import. | Import rejects blank, whitespace-only or non-numeric marks and blank BITS ID/Course, with Excel row numbers (first 10 shown, plus "…and N more"). | Mocked and real `.xls`: `Row 5: Total Marks "" is not a number`, `Row 6: Total Marks "AB"…`. | **#19** Bell curve was shifted sideways relative to the bars. | Marks centred at 95: curve peak at x=315, bar centre at x=330. | Bars used 32 px per 10 marks; the curve used 30 px. | One shared geometry: `markToX()` maps each bin's centre mark to its bar's centre. | Mean 95: curve peak exactly over the 90–100 bar centre.
